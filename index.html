Pressure Board

Pressure = how much a ball's possible outcomes can swing the batting side's chance of winning, compared with an average ball. A ball is a pressure ball at 1.5 times average or more.

Why we rank output, not improvement

The brief asks how players perform under pressure compared with their usual level. We measured that gap for every player, then checked whether it repeats. Split each player's matches into odd and even games and correlate the two halves: 0 means luck, 1 means a fixed skill.

The gap above a player's own normal barely repeats. How much he produces under pressure, against an average player facing the same balls, repeats about three times as well. So that is what the lists rank, with the player's usual level steadying small samples.
